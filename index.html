About Me

I'm Ian Stokes — a Chartered Engineer with an MSc from Oxford and over three decades of experience turning ambitious engineering concepts into commercial reality.

As Head of Strategy & Technology at Inductosense, I've helped scale the company from a PhD research concept to a commercial enterprise serving the world's largest energy companies. Our IoT sensor technology enables predictive maintenance and asset integrity monitoring for critical infrastructure.

I serve as strategic advisor to our CTO, bridging the gap between cutting-edge technology and market needs. My role combines hands-on technical leadership with high-level strategic thinking — exactly where I thrive.

Beyond the boardroom, I'm a maker at heart. I run three Bambu P1S 3D printers, create AI-generated artwork, and constantly tinker with mechanical design projects. I believe the best leaders never stop building things.
